## URTH brief (recap, verbatim from user)

The four explicit must-haves:

1. **Filter in hand** — held up, catching light, ring markings legible. Shallow DOF, hand as scale.
2. **POV screwing filter onto lens** — first-person, hands-only.
3. **Closeup of filter threading onto lens** — macro-ish, threads meeting. The "tactile precision" shot.
4. **Photographer taking the shot** — camera to eye, filter visibly mounted. Environmental, not posed.

Bonus context: filter/gear with **URTH Helios** backpack (unreleased), tripod + Helios + Pro filter as *kit in landscape*, bag-open showing organization.

Landscape pillars: harsh real conditions; early starts, long walks, waiting; earned vantages; minimalist; monochrome OK; human element optional. Each landscape should ideally **prove a filter is doing work** (CPL on water/glass, ND on motion, ND+Black Mist on harsh light).

Target split (URTH's): 3 product · 3 landscape · 2 wildcard = 8 deliverables. **The current cull is 17 frames** — broader than the URTH deliverable; this preview essay shows the work, not the final 8.

User constraint: **keep the product shots together** in the sequence.
