## One day.

Village to summit to dark.

- 12:00 Depart: Leave the retreat. Drive south toward Aït Bougmez.
- 14:00 Happy Valley Gorge: Bassouz meets the group. Mule, donkeys, slot canyon.
- 15:00 AGA Mountain Field: Open terrain. Four frames. The weakest light of the day.
- 16:00 Tsiki Pass: Switchbacks, scree, ridgeline. The country opens.
- 18:30 Sunset: Golden hour on the pass. Two-figure silhouettes.
- 19:30 Blue hour: Speedlight and headlamp. Flash on rock spires.
- 21:30 Return: Hammam and hot tub. The day is done.
